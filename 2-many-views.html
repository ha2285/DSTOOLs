Charts this table can't show, and why

        
Line, area, slope and log-scale chartsThey need a time column (date, year, season). This table has none. sns.lineplot(data=df, x="date", y="value")

        
Pair plotShows every pair of numeric columns at once, so each penguin appears in many panels, not as one dot. sns.pairplot(df, hue="species")

        
TreemapBest for many categories or a deep hierarchy; here there are only 3 islands × 3 species. squarify.plot(sizes=counts, label=names)
