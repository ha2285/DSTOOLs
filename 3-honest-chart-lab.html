      Rules that apply to every chart
      
          
      
            
      Start from the question
      Decide what the reader should compare or notice, then pick the chart.
      
            
      Title the takeaway
      "IT pays the most" tells readers what to see. "Salary by department" does not.
      
            
      Label axes with units
      Years, $, kg. Say when an axis is log-scaled or doesn't start at zero.
      
            
      Zero baseline for length and area
      Bars and areas start at zero. Dots and lines may zoom in.
      
            
      Color with meaning
      Categorical palettes for groups, sequential for order, diverging when a midpoint matters. Prefer colorblind-safe palettes.
      
            
      Show the data behind summaries
      Pair bars of means, boxes and trend lines with the raw points when the sample allows.
      
            
      Matplotlib for control, seaborn for statistics
      Seaborn returns matplotlib objects, so you can always finish a seaborn chart with plt.title(), plt.xlabel() and so on.
      
            
      Check what AI-written code drew
      Generated charts often default to truncated axes, unsorted bars or misleading colormaps. Review before you share.
